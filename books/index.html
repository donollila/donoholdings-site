The Warm Stone · Book One

Dream Dive

Don Ollie

It’s warm only for the one it chooses.

Charlie Taylor cuts limestone in Bloomington, Indiana. He is twenty-four, out of shape, and spends most nights on the same barstool at the Green Tuxedo. Then a block of stone splits open at the quarry, and inside it, in a hollow no bigger than his fist, lies a necklace: a black oval pendant swirled with gold.

It’s warm. And that night, Charlie dreams he is swimming.

The man who never learned to swim starts getting up at 4:30 in the morning to train. He sheds the weight, races open water, and meets Jeanie Chrisman, a swimmer who sees something in him no one else ever has. But the dream keeps calling him farther: to the flooded caves of Mexico, and then to a sea cliff on the coast of Turkey, where something has been waiting for a very long time.

About the author

Don Ollie has been swimming since he was fourteen, when he showed up to his first high school swim practice in a pair of soccer shorts and no goggles. He has been in the water ever since.

Years later, in his early thirties, a dream kept coming back to him. It was always the same dream. One night he woke up and finally wrote it down in a notebook. An outline followed soon after, and over the years that outline grew into Dream Dive, his first novel and the first book in The Warm Stone series.

By day, Don is an IT consultant. He shares his home with his daughter, Bob the cat, and Ruby the golden retriever.
