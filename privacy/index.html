SoliTrio Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: SoliTrio doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information. Your games, stats, settings and photos stay on your iPhone.

Who we are

SoliTrio is a solitaire game for iPhone made by Dono Holdings (“we” or “us”). This policy covers the SoliTrio app and this website. If you have a question about it, email support@donoholdings.com.

What SoliTrio collects

Nothing. SoliTrio has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t include any third-party code that collects data. The app doesn’t send anything to us; we don’t run servers for it.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, SoliTrio keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • Games in progress and the Daily Challenge you’re playing
  • Your wins, streaks and records
  • Your settings, such as table color, card style, auto-move and auto-finish
  • A resized copy of any photo you choose for the table background or card backs

This information never leaves your iPhone except as part of your own device backups (iCloud or your computer), according to your iPhone’s settings. Deleting SoliTrio deletes it.

Photos and camera

SoliTrio only uses a photo when you choose one in Settings. If you take a new photo, iOS asks for your permission to use the camera first. The photo is stored only inside SoliTrio on your iPhone and is never uploaded.

Subscriptions and payments

The SoliTrio Annual subscription is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription; we never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information. SoliTrio only asks Apple whether your subscription is active. Apple’s handling of your information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

SoliTrio is suitable for all ages and doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it doesn’t load anything from other companies. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site running and secure. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here and update the effective date above.
