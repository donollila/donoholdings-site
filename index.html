SoliTrio

Every deal can be won

Klondike, FreeCell and Spider in one calm, beautifully simple solitaire app.

See the screenshots Support & FAQ

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

♠Three games, one app

The classic Klondike, all-cards-showing FreeCell and two-deck Spider, switchable in a tap.

♥Every deal winnable

Each hand is checked by a built-in solver before it’s dealt, so you’re never stuck with an impossible game.

♦“Show me how”

Stuck? SoliTrio replays a winning line one move at a time. Stop whenever you like and carry on from there.

♣Play your way

Draw one or three in Klondike. One, two or four suits in Spider. Unlimited undo.

♠Wins and streaks

Track your wins and best streak for each game, and replay any deal you want another crack at.

♥Private and offline

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your stats stay on your phone, and it plays without a connection.

A closer look

Big, readable cards on a green felt table, with every control in easy reach of your thumb.

About the studio

Dono Holdings is a one-person app studio based in Tualatin, Oregon. It’s run by a software consultant who has spent decades building systems for large companies, and who wanted to make a few small things that are simply pleasant to use.

SoliTrio is the first. More apps are on the way.
