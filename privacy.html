Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version: our apps don’t collect, store or share any personal information. Your game data stays on your device.

Who we are

This policy covers apps published by Dono Holdings (“we”, “us”), including SoliTrio, and this website, donoholdings.com.

Information we collect

None. SoliTrio does not ask for an account, your name, your email address or any other personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools of any kind, and it does not contain third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data stored on your device

SoliTrio saves a small amount of information on your iPhone so the app works the way you expect:

  • Your game in progress, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Your settings (for example, Klondike draw count and Spider suits)
  • Your win counts and streaks

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children’s privacy

Because our apps collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children under 13.

This website

donoholdings.com does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability; we do not use these to identify you.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we’ll use your email address and message only to reply to you. We won’t add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, for example if a future app needs to handle data differently, we’ll update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@donoholdings.com.
