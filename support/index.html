Common questions

How does the subscription work?

SoliTrio Annual starts with a 7-day free trial. When the trial ends, $9.99 is charged to your Apple Account, then again once a year until you cancel. The subscription unlocks everything: all three games, the Daily Challenge, hints, Show Me How and custom looks.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose SoliTrio and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also open SoliTrio, tap the gear icon, and choose Manage subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or renewal date to avoid being charged. You keep SoliTrio until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in with your Apple Account, find SoliTrio, and choose Request a refund.

I subscribed, but SoliTrio still asks me to subscribe

Tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen (or in Settings under Subscription). Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with and is connected to the internet. If it still doesn’t work, email us.

Can I use SoliTrio on more than one iPhone?

Yes. Your subscription works on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account; tap Restore purchases on the new one. Games and stats are kept on each iPhone separately and don’t sync between devices.

Is every deal really winnable?

Before each Klondike, FreeCell and Spider (1 or 2 suit) deal, SoliTrio’s solver plays it through to confirm a winning line exists. 4-suit Spider deals are too complex to check in advance, so they’re dealt at random. If you get stuck, tap Hint, or Show Me How to watch a winning line.

When does the Daily Challenge change?

At midnight in your time zone. Everyone gets the same winnable deal that day. Solve it on consecutive days to grow your daily streak.

Will I lose my stats if I delete SoliTrio?

Yes. Your games, stats and photos are stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes them. They are included in your iPhone’s backups, so restoring a backup brings them back.

Does SoliTrio need an internet connection?

No. SoliTrio works fully offline. It only needs a connection to start or restore your subscription with the App Store.

How do I use my own photo?

Tap the gear icon next to the game name, then under Look choose Add photo for the table or the card backs. Tap it again later to change it.
