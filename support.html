SoliTrio FAQ

Is every game really winnable?

Yes, for Klondike, FreeCell and one- or two-suit Spider. Before each deal, SoliTrio’s built-in solver checks that a winning line exists. Four-suit Spider is much harder to solve, so those deals aren’t guaranteed.

How do I switch games?

Tap the game name at the top left of the table (for example, “Klondike”). Pick Klondike, FreeCell or Spider, and set your Klondike draw and Spider suit options in the same panel.

What does “Show me how” do?

It resets the current deal and plays out a winning line one move at a time. Tap Stop at any point to take over from there. A hand you’ve watched this way doesn’t count as a win, and it ends your current streak.

How do I move cards?

Drag a card or a run of cards to where you want it, or just tap it and SoliTrio will move it to the best available spot. When the game can finish itself, a Finish button appears.

Can I replay a deal?

Yes. Tap New game and choose “Replay this deal”, or pick it from the screen that appears after a win.

Will I lose my stats if I switch phones?

Your wins and streaks are stored only on your device, so they don’t move to a new phone. Restoring a full iPhone backup will usually bring them along.

Does SoliTrio need an internet connection?

No. It plays entirely offline.

Does SoliTrio collect my data?

No. There are no ads, accounts or analytics. See our Privacy Policy for details.
